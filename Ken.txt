Ken Thompson is an American computer scientist best known as one of the creators of Unix and an early contributor to the C programming language.
In the late 1960s at Bell Labs, he helped design Unix as a small, clean operating system that was easy to understand and modify,
which was a big shift from the heavy systems of the time.
He later co-created the Go programming language at Google. Thompson is known for valuing simplicity and elegance in software,
and his ideas quietly shaped how modern operating systems and programming languages work.

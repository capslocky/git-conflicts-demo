Margaret Hamilton is an American software engineer who led the team behind the onboard software for NASA’s Apollo missions.
Back in the 1960s, when software was often treated as an afterthought,
she insisted it needed the same discipline as hardware and helped coin the term “software engineering.”
The systems her team built were designed to handle errors gracefully,
which famously helped save the Apollo 11 Moon landing when the computer was overloaded.
For a long time her work flew under the radar, but she’s now recognized as a major figure in the history of computing.

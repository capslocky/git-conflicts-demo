Margaret Hamilton is an American software engineer best known for leading the team that wrote the onboard software for NASA’s Apollo missions.
In the 1960s, when software wasn’t taken very seriously,
she pushed for rigorous engineering practices and helped invent what we now call “software engineering.”
Her work on error detection and recovery played a key role in safely landing humans on the Moon, even when things went wrong during Apollo 11.
She stayed relatively unknown for years, but today she’s widely recognized as one of the people who helped make modern software engineering possible.
